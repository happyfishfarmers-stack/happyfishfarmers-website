Feed is your largest variable cost. Sustainable farming is not “less feed” — it is **less waste** and **better conversion**.

## Feed curves beat vibes

Shift protein levels and meal sizes by growth stage. Random extras usually sink before fish catch them.

## Watch bottom behavior

Waste feed feeds algae blooms and bad mornings. If fish are not competitively eating, pause before you add more.

## Pair nutrition with records

Track growth checks weekly. Numbers make adjustments obvious — and training accelerates how you read them.
