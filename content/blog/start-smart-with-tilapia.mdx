The fastest way to lose momentum on a new pond is guessing. Tilapia rewards clarity: **where water comes from**, **how it leaves**, and **how many fish the pond can carry when the weather turns**.

## Start with water balance

Before construction, map your dry season. If inflow drops, aeration and partial harvest plans matter more than feed brand debates.

## Stock for learning first

Smaller initial stocking lets you read behavior, feeding response, and mortality signals without betting the whole batch.

## Train before you scale

Pair reading with structured lessons — water tests, fertilization timing, and feed curves make sense faster when you see them together.
